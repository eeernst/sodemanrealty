Fall Seller Playbook: Price to the Buyer in Front of You

How to set ask price when inventory is up and automatic bidding wars are less automatic.

Quick take

  • Fall buyers are decisive but selective — they walk when the ask ignores recent solds.
  • A small, early price correction usually beats three weeks of silence and a larger cut later.
  • Condition and clarity (disclosures, repairs already done) protect more of the net than a stretch ask.

For sellers

  • Pull solds from the last 60–90 days in your school district / price band before listing.
  • Decide your walk-away net before day one on market, then price so the first weekend still draws traffic.
  • If showings are light after week one, revisit price or presentation immediately — don’t wait for month two.

For buyers watching fall listings

  • Price drops are data, not always desperation; ask what changed (comps, inspection fear, timing).
  • Strong offers still win the right house: clean terms, realistic appraisal path, and a clear closing plan.
