August Snapshot: Rebalancing, Not a Reset

More listings and cooler price growth — still a seller-leaning market if the home is priced to today.

Quick take

  • Mid-2026 local reports pointed to more active inventory year over year and months of supply edging up — still below a balanced 5–6 month market.
  • Median sale prices looked roughly flat versus the double-digit spring headlines earlier in the year.
  • Homes priced to current comps still sell; homes priced to last spring’s trendline sit.

For first-time buyers

  • You may see more to compare this fall — use that for diligence, not for lowballing every listing.
  • Prioritize condition and location; a “deal” with expensive surprises is not a win.

For sellers

  • Price from fresh solds in your pocket of Mid-Michigan, not from what a neighbor asked last April.
  • Prep and presentation still separate a 2–3 week sale from a 45+ day stall.

Not financial advice; for informational purposes. Figures move monthly — treat this as context, not a forecast.
