  <h2>Quick take</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Fall buyers are decisive but selective — they walk when the ask ignores recent solds.</li>
    <li>A small, early price correction usually beats three weeks of silence and a larger cut later.</li>
    <li>Condition and clarity (disclosures, repairs already done) protect more of the net than a stretch ask.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>For sellers</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Pull solds from the last 60–90 days in your school district / price band before listing.</li>
    <li>Decide your walk-away net <em>before</em> day one on market, then price so the first weekend still draws traffic.</li>
    <li>If showings are light after week one, revisit price or presentation immediately — don’t wait for month two.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>For buyers watching fall listings</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Price drops are data, not always desperation; ask what changed (comps, inspection fear, timing).</li>
    <li>Strong offers still win the right house: clean terms, realistic appraisal path, and a clear closing plan.</li>
  </ul>
