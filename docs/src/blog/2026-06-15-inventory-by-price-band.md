  <h2>Quick take</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Greater Lansing inventory has been rebuilding unevenly — more choice in some bands, still thin in others.</li>
    <li>Well-presented homes in the most searched price ranges still move; overpriced listings linger.</li>
    <li>Strategy starts with <em>your</em> band, not a metro headline.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>For first-time buyers</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Track how many actives and pendings sit in your ceiling range for 2–3 weeks before writing.</li>
    <li>If your band is still competitive, keep financing solid and timelines clean; if it is softer, ask for repairs or credits instead of stretching price.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>For investors</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Underwrite to the rent and condition reality of that band — not metro median sale price.</li>
    <li>Favor deals where vacancy and deferred maintenance are priced in before you bid.</li>
  </ul>
