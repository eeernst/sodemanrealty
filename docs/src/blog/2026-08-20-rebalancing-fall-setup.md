  <h2>Quick take</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Mid-2026 local reports pointed to more active inventory year over year and months of supply edging up — still below a balanced 5–6 month market.</li>
    <li>Median sale prices looked roughly flat versus the double-digit spring headlines earlier in the year.</li>
    <li>Homes priced to <em>current</em> comps still sell; homes priced to last spring’s trendline sit.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>For first-time buyers</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>You may see more to compare this fall — use that for diligence, not for lowballing every listing.</li>
    <li>Prioritize condition and location; a “deal” with expensive surprises is not a win.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>For sellers</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Price from fresh solds in your pocket of Mid-Michigan, not from what a neighbor asked last April.</li>
    <li>Prep and presentation still separate a 2–3 week sale from a 45+ day stall.</li>
  </ul>
